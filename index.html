Christmas Dance Field Notes

Observations from one household's December, kept as short notes. Subject: the family's adoption of the Christmas dance video generator.

Note 1 — the adoption curve

Week one: one clip, sent as a joke, from the photo dance tool. Week two: three households producing. Week three: my aunt asks for "the website with the dancing" in all lowercase. Adoption took eleven days.

Note 2 — the pet variable

Every household with an animal eventually produces an animal clip. The pet stage is the most requested and the least argued about. Cats outperform dogs in reply rate, contrary to all household polling.

Note 3 — the couple format

The two-photo couple clip is the format that converts skeptics. One clip, both partners, unmistakably us — relatives respond to the pairing, not the dancing.

Note 4 — the cost, once more

Free preview, paid export, one-off. The comparison page says it better than I can. The guide remains the only documentation anyone actually needed.

End of notes. Unofficial fan page for christmas-dance.net.
